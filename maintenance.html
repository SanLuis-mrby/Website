Maintenance and Prevention

The best way to fix a computer problem is to stop it from happening. A few simple habits, done regularly, keep your computer fast, safe, and reliable for years.

Maintenance Schedule

How Often Task
Daily Shut down or restart properly; do not just close the laptop lid for weeks
Weekly Install updates, empty the Recycle Bin, check that antivirus is on
Monthly Back up files, delete temporary files, uninstall unused programs
Every 3 to 6 months Clean dust from the keyboard, vents, and fans
Yearly Review passwords, check the health of your hard drive, clean the inside of a desktop

Back Up Your Files

Hard drives can fail, laptops can be stolen, and viruses can destroy data. A backup is a second copy of your important files stored somewhere else. Experts recommend the 3-2-1 rule:

  • 3 copies of your data
  • 2 different types of storage (for example, your computer and an external drive)
  • 1 copy stored away from home, such as cloud storage

Keep Software Updated

  • Turn on automatic updates for Windows.
  • Update your browser and its extensions.
  • Update drivers for graphics, sound, and network devices.
  • Updates fix bugs and close security holes that attackers use.

Clean Your Computer

Outside

  1. Shut down and unplug the computer.
  2. Wipe the screen with a soft, dry microfiber cloth. Never spray liquid directly onto the screen.
  3. Turn the keyboard upside down and gently shake it, then use compressed air between the keys.
  4. Wipe the case and mouse with a slightly damp cloth.

Inside (desktop)

  1. Turn off and unplug the computer, and touch a metal part of the case to discharge static.
  2. Open the side panel.
  3. Use short bursts of compressed air to blow dust out of the fans and vents. Hold the fan blades still.
  4. Do not use a vacuum cleaner inside the case, because it can create static electricity.

Take Care of Your Laptop Battery

  • Avoid leaving the laptop in a hot car or in direct sunlight.
  • Do not let the battery drop to 0% often.
  • If the laptop stays plugged in most of the time, use the battery care or charge limit setting if available.
  • Use the original or a compatible charger.

When to Call a Professional

  • The hard drive makes clicking or grinding sounds.
  • There is liquid damage.
  • The computer smells burnt or you see smoke. Unplug it immediately.
  • You have tried the steps in this guide and the problem remains.
  • You are not comfortable opening the computer.

Summary: Update regularly, back up your files, keep the computer clean and cool, use antivirus, and be careful about what you click and download.
